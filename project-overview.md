# Project Overview

Project Name: Historical Echoes: Chat with Legends (Mock AI)

Description: Immerse yourself in history with Historical Echoes! This web application *simulates* conversations with iconic figures from the past. Ask Marie Curie about her discoveries, seek wisdom from Marcus Aurelius, or explore art with Leonardo da Vinci. Experience the illusion of interacting with history, all within a beautifully designed and user-friendly interface.

Features:

*   Selectable Historical Figures: Choose from a curated roster of historical figures, each with a distinct "personality" and knowledge base (Marie Curie, Marcus Aurelius, Leonardo da Vinci).
*   Simulated Chat Interface: Engage in conversations through a chat interface. Ask questions or seek advice and receive responses crafted to reflect the figure's known views and expertise.
*   Interaction Modes: Explore different interaction modes, including:
    *   Q&A: Ask direct questions about their life, work, and era.
    *   Advice: Seek guidance on personal or philosophical matters.
*   Biographical Insights: Learn more about each figure through concise biographical summaries presented alongside the chat interface.

Target Audience: History enthusiasts, students, lifelong learners, and anyone curious about the past.

Technology Stack:

*   Frontend: Next.js on Cloudflare Pages for a fast, responsive, and engaging user experience.
